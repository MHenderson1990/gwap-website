    MOGWAP

    Hip Hop/R&B · Dallas,TX

    Born and raised in Dallas, TX, Mo Gwap has been crafting his sound since 2009. Blending hip-hop and R&B into something uniquely his own, Mo Gwap draws inspiration from the greats — New Edition, Earth Wind & Fire, Dru Hill, N'Sync, UGK, Drake, J. Cole, Doja Cat and Chris Brown, just to name a few — while carving out a lane that's entirely his. His music is evolving into a grown and sexy sound built for late nights and good energy. When you press play, expect to feel good, stay chill, and ride the vibe.
